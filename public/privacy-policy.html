3. How we share information

  • Service providers: We may use hosting, payment, analytics, or other vendors who process data on our instructions.
  • When you share: Content you share via links or exports may be visible to people who receive those links.
  • Legal: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion of your account subject to our verification and any legal retention requirements.
